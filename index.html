Recipe #1: My lovely, deliscious, Scrumpdillyicious, mouth watering Cheesecake

  • Time: _
  • Serves: _
    Two Crusts Work for this quantity of things.
Ingredients Quantity
Cream Cheese 32 oz (3 blocks)
Sweetened Condensed Milk 14 oz (1 can)
Grandulated Sugar 1/4 cup
Sour Cream 1/2 cup
Vanilla Extract 2 tsp
Zest and Juice of Lemon 1/2
All Purpose Flower 1/4 cup
Eggs. ROOM TEMPERATURE 4 of them.
pinchy of salty

Step 1: Put the Cream cheese (room temperature), Sweetened condensed milk, granulated sugar, sour cream, zest of lemon and juice, all purpose flower, eggs, and pinch of salt and mix all together.

Step 2: Preheat oven to 350 degrees

Then yeet your mixture in the oven for a good 45 mins - 1 hour.
